FAQ

Straight answers.

Still have a question? Ask it when we talk, or email quotasaleshq@gmail.com.

Do I need sales experience?

No. We train you on the products, the pitch and handling objections, and you'll do ride-alongs in the field before you knock on your own. What matters is drive and being coachable.

Is this commission-only?

Yes. It's 100% commission with no base salary. Earning potential is uncapped, and what you earn depends on what you sell, your market, your activity and your results.

How does compensation work?

Compensation varies by product, number of lines or accounts, market and the applicable compensation structure. Eligible sales and installations are paid according to the applicable compensation schedule, generally weekly. Bonuses and incentives may be available. We walk through the full details during the hiring process.

What exactly will I sell?

Fiber internet, wireless and telecom services from leading providers, door to door. Fiber is our biggest opportunity right now. Available products and providers vary by market.

Can I choose my market?

We have opportunities in many states. You'll have input into where you work, and managers help place reps in strong markets where they can succeed.

Are there opportunities to travel?

Yes. Reps work markets across the country. When travel opportunities are available, management helps coordinate and support the move into active markets.

Are there set hours?

No set hours. You're building your own income, so how much you put in is up to you, and the more you work, the more earning potential you have. Your manager will walk you through the expectations for your team and market.

Is there a path into leadership?

Yes. Strong reps can build and lead their own teams, grow into management and earn additional income through team production and applicable overrides. Advancement is based on performance and fit.

How do I get started?

Fill out the short application below. A member of our team will reach out to talk through the role, the markets and next steps. Your engagement structure and applicable classification will be explained during the hiring process.
